Ask a compatibility question ↗

Desktop & laptop

Open the downloaded HTML in a modern browser. Edit your trip, use the planner’s save controls, and reopen your saved copy to check your changes. Keep backups of important plans.

iPhone & iPad

Native Files access is view-only. Editing requires a compatible third-party HTML viewer that supports interactive HTML and saving. Viewer capabilities vary; check your app before relying on it.

Android

Open the file in a browser or HTML viewer that supports interactive HTML. Saving behavior varies by app. Test saving and reopening an edited copy on your device.

Good to know

A few practical answers.

Is this a PDF or a spreadsheet?

It’s an interactive HTML file with its own interface. Open it in a compatible browser or viewer, edit your plans, and save an updated copy.

Can everyone edit the same trip at once?

There’s no shared real-time editing. Share saved copies with your group and agree on who maintains the current version.

Does it work without internet?

Core trip planning works offline. Maps, online links, AI services, and some external fonts and imagery require internet. Keep essential addresses and confirmations available separately for travel.

Does the planner generate an AI itinerary?

Use an external assistant of your choice with the downloadable format instructions. The planner imports a supported JSON itinerary; it doesn’t include a live AI generator. Your chosen service’s terms and availability apply.
